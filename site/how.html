Why a cloud agent

Not a single prompt to a model. A real agent that lives on Omnara, uses tools, and keeps its memory.

1One agent per account

Every judgement starts a fresh agent on Omnara. They run side by side in the cloud, not on our server.

2It investigates

The agent decides what to read: your best posts, your flops, your replies. It sorts your posts into topics before it scores anything.

3It sticks around

The agent doesn't end when the report is done. Ask it something on your report page and it answers with the full context of your account.

How the score works

Build your own

The judge is one Omnara agent profile with three custom tools. Custom tools aren't run by Omnara: when the agent calls one, our server gets the call and sends back the answer through the Omnara API.

That's the whole pattern. Your app keeps the data and the side effects, and Omnara runs the agent.
